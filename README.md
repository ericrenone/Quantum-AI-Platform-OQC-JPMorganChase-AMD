## Overview

OQC, JPMorganChase, and AMD have launched a **strategic research collaboration** in London to build and operate a first-of-its-kind **Quantum-AI Data Centre** — a unified environment where quantum processors, AI models, and high-performance computing infrastructure are integrated under one roof.

The goal is direct: move quantum-enabled business applications from theoretical promise to real-world deployment, starting with the computational challenges that are hardest for classical systems — portfolio optimization, risk modeling, and large-scale machine learning.

## The Platform

At the center of the collaboration is a **Quantum-AI Data Centre** built and operated by OQC in London. The facility provides a dedicated, secure, enterprise-grade environment designed for three things:

1. **Test** hybrid quantum-classical computing models in realistic conditions
2. **Evaluate** near-term quantum advantage across financial workloads
3. **Accelerate** the path from research prototype to production-ready application

The facility is not a proof-of-concept. It is purpose-built infrastructure intended to become a reusable model for how organizations across industries integrate quantum and AI technologies.

---

## Research Focus Areas

### 🏦 Financial Services
JPMorganChase researchers will use the platform to investigate quantum and hybrid quantum-classical applications with direct financial relevance:

- **Portfolio optimization** — finding optimal asset allocations across large, constrained solution spaces
- **Risk assessment** — modeling tail risk, scenario simulation, and stress testing at scale
- **Market simulation** — Monte Carlo and path-dependent pricing at Heisenberg-limit efficiency
- **Machine learning workloads** — quantum-enhanced feature spaces and kernel methods

### 🔬 Foundational Research
The collaboration also targets open research questions about hybrid computing architecture:

- How do quantum processors and classical AI accelerators best divide labor?
- Where does quantum advantage first become commercially meaningful?
- What software abstractions make hybrid systems accessible to domain researchers?

## Key Applications

JPMorganChase has existing quantum algorithms across four domains that this platform is designed to extend and validate:

| Domain | Quantum Approach | Classical Baseline |
|---|---|---|
| Option pricing | Quantum amplitude estimation | Monte Carlo simulation |
| Portfolio management | Quantum optimization (QAOA / VQE) | Integer programming |
| Risk analysis | Quantum Monte Carlo methods | Variance reduction techniques |
| Machine learning | Quantum kernels, QNNs | Classical neural networks |

## Why This Matters

Demand for advanced analytics and AI-driven decision-making in financial services is outpacing what conventional computing can efficiently deliver. Three structural shifts are converging:

- **Scale** — portfolio sizes, derivative books, and simulation spaces are growing faster than Moore's Law
- **Regulation** — real-time risk reporting requirements demand faster, more accurate scenario engines
- **AI integration** — ML workloads increasingly benefit from hybrid quantum-classical computation

This collaboration sits at the intersection of all three. By combining quantum hardware, AI acceleration, and a financial-domain research agenda in a single purpose-built facility, the partnership represents one of the most direct attempts yet to close the gap between quantum computing research and production financial infrastructure.
